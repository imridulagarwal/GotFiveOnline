📖 How to Play Got Five!

Goal: Deduce the 5 hidden numbers on your stand! You have 1 tile of each of the 5 colors, arranged in ascending order from left to right.

The 60 Tiles: Numbers 1 to 60 cycle through the 5 colors (Blue ends in 1/6, Pink in 2/7, Yellow in 3/8, Green in 4/9, Orange in 5/0). Each tile also has 1, 2, or 3 dots.

Multiplayer & Turn Timers: Every visitor gets an automatic random Player ID. Create a room for 2–4 players, customize each seat's turn timer, and tap 🔗 Copy Invite Link to invite friends on any phone, tablet, or computer!

On Your Turn:

  1. Reveal a Tile: Tap any colored Supply Stack to flip 1 tile face-up into the Center Pool.
  2. Ask for a Clue: Pick any 1 tile from the Face-Up Pool and choose either:
    • ↕️ Sort: Places that tile into one of the 6 notches around your stand, showing where its number fits among yours.
    • •• Compare: Asks if that tile has the same number of dots (1, 2, or 3) as one of your 5 hidden tiles.
