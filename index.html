📖 How to Play Got Five!

Page 1 — Guest Entry: Enter as a Guest with an auto-generated ID (`PLR-XXXX`).

Page 2 — Public Lobby: Browse available rooms and online players, or create a new room (with custom or untimed turns and host-controlled Auto-Deduce).

Room Ready Pop-Up: When you create or join a room, the Host can copy the invite link and add/remove AI bots. Everyone clicks Ready to start!

Page 3 — Game Page: Flip a supply tile, ask a Sort or Compare clue, mark possibilities (`◯`) on your sheet, and shout "GOT FIVE!" to win. Afterward, replay in the same room or exit to the lobby!
